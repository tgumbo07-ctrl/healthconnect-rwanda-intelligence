Agu Precious - Data Analytics cross-track feature review (1 November 2026)

Supported:
- Gender
- HasChronicCondition
- VisitType
- Age, with the caveat that it should be calculated as of the appointment date.

Retain with caveats:
- FacilityID
- DepartmentID
The Data Analytics validation found no statistically significant differences in facility/department ratings, but that does not establish that the IDs have no predictive value. Test separately and assess generalisation.

Require further validation:
- MaritalStatus
- Education
- BloodGroup
- BMI

Hold pending prediction-time clarification:
- Diagnosis
- Severity
These may be recorded during or after clinical assessment and could introduce leakage depending on the prediction point.

Suggested time-safe additions:
- PriorVisits, calculated only from appointments before the current appointment
- Appointment month
- Appointment weekday

Data limitation:
- The Appointments sheet has no attendance/no-show field.

Prediction-point question:
- Clarify whether prediction occurs at booking, patient arrival, or after initial clinical assessment.

Data Science decision:
- For the Week 7 cross-track re-test, the prediction point is defined as patient arrival / before initial clinical assessment.
